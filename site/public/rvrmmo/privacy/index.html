RVRMMO Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

RVRMMO is a game in a small, invitation-only private test run by Haki Tools. This page explains what the game collects, why, and how long it is kept.

What we collect

  • Your Epic Games account identifier and display name. You sign in with Epic Games. Epic tells us your account identifier and display name; we never see your Epic password.
  • Game data. Your characters (names, realm, appearance), their gear, inventory, progression, guild membership and position in the game world.
  • Your public IP address. You give it to us so the test server lets you connect, and the server records it in its connection logs.
  • Chat messages you send in the game, which pass through our chat server to other players.
  • Technical logs from the game server (connections, errors) used to fix bugs.

Why we use it

Only to run the test: to sign you in, save your characters, let you connect, deliver chat, and find and fix problems. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone except the services that host the game (Amazon Web Services, where the servers and backups run, and Epic Games for sign-in).

How long we keep it

Game data is kept for the length of the private test. Backups are deleted automatically after 30 days. When the test ends, or if you ask us to, we delete your account and characters.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see or delete your data, or to remove your IP address from the server's list. Contact us through haki.tools.

Children

The private test is for adults invited by us. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above, and tell testers before the next play session.
